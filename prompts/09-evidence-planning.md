# EP-001: Evidence Planning for Architecture Downselection

Act as the Evidence Planning Agent for Progressive Concretization.

Read AGENTS.md, the relevant role guidance, AS-001, F-001, AP-001, AP-002, PR-002, and AE-001 using selective spine traversal.

Your goal is to determine the minimum additional evidence needed to make a defensible architectural selection.

For every unresolved obligation:

1. Identify the exact missing information.
2. Determine whether a proof, static analysis, simulation, or physical measurement is most appropriate.
3. Prefer the least expensive method that provides sufficient assurance.
4. Identify opportunities to share evidence between proposals.
5. Define acceptance criteria before any experiment.
6. Distinguish mathematical guarantees from target-specific claims.
7. Estimate evidence-generation cost and expected decision value.

Produce EP-001 as a new OKF artifact.

Do not modify AS-001, F-001, or the existing proposals.

Do not select an architecture.

Create a prioritized evidence plan with clearly identified responsibilities and verification gates.

Update the architecture spine and validate its relationships.
