# VO-004-v2 candidate: corrected R-001

**Disposition:** Proposed rescoping only. Historical VO-004 and the current
VO ledger remain unchanged. This candidate cannot discharge VO-004 or
supersede its authoritative state before independent AS review and required
authority/ledger update.

**Detector obligation:** For each legal sample accepted at the detector
interface, perform exactly one sample-indexed update; the numerical result
must not depend on elapsed time between invocations.

**Evidence:** At AP/CON, review the exact accepted-sample boundary and a
bounded per-call work argument, without imposing a numerical call-rate
requirement. At implementation, inspect input dispatch and update behavior
for one-to-one accepted-sample processing. This does not prove physical
publisher continuity or unlimited throughput.

**External publisher assumption:** The upstream publisher supplies the
intended ordered published sequence without missing, duplicate, or reordered
records at the detector boundary. Assign verification of that interface
promise to an approved integration owner if/when an integration contract is
defined; no such physical evidence is present in this task's inputs.

**Conditional separate obligation:** If engineering authority later approves
a throughput, overload, or real-time requirement, create a separately
identified integration requirement and VO with its own scope, owner, due
stage, and acceptance evidence. No such obligation is asserted here.

**Acceptance:** Independent review of this corrected scope and, after
approval, appropriate implementation evidence. Structural checks do not
establish functional correctness. Existing VO-002, VO-003, VO-005 and VO-006
remain open; VO-004 remains open pending accepted supersession.
