# AE-003 — Controlled provisional architecture selection

## Decision

**NO_DOWNSELECTION.** Neither candidate currently clears all AP-stage obligations. In particular, VO-001 is due at AS and has no accepted independent receipt. The existing reconciliation receipt explicitly leaves it pending; the stage gate correctly rejects that obligation as unresolved. AP-001 also lacks its own AP-stage exactness and overflow argument. No completed firmware, linker map, or measured target WCET was required for this decision.

The C-002 authority chain is preserved: approved Option B (`C-002-SCOPE-001`) retains the 2048-byte detector-attributable SRAM ceiling. Both candidate envelopes allocate 904 bytes across seven categories and reserve 1024 bytes, totaling 1928 bytes and leaving 120 additional unallocated bytes. These are proposed allocation caps, not measured or proven physical consumption. Their feasibility is conditional on the stated execution, attribution, overlap, and enforcement assumptions; VO-002 remains open until implementation evidence is independently accepted.

## F-001 projection

F-001 is unchanged and requires feasibility before comparing its Pareto dimensions. The current projections are not a supported ranking:

| Candidate | State SRAM bytes | Worst-case cycles per update | Flash bytes |
|---|---|---|---|
| AP-001 | 72-byte conditional allocation estimate | Unknown; bounded at most 32 square/add terms plus loop/control, no target-cycle claim | Unknown |
| AP-002 | 72-byte conditional allocation estimate | Unknown; fixed bounded recurrence workload, no target-cycle claim | Unknown |

The 1928-byte charged C-002 envelope is distinct from F-001 state SRAM. With cycles and flash unknown and AP obligations unresolved, there is no Pareto winner and no authorized tradeoff. If the evidence later makes the candidates incomparable, propose an authority decision and associated AD record before committing; AD-003 is only proposed in this evaluation and both assessment packets, not accepted or added to the ledger. No authority action selecting an AP is implied.

## Minimal evidence to reopen the gate

1. Obtain an independent, explicit accepted receipt for VO-001 that determines whether BKP-ADC-001 is adequate authority for the exact C-001 input-domain claim. If not, provide the required authority/source and repeat that review.
2. For AP-001, provide a candidate-specific exactness and universal no-overflow argument covering the 32-sample zero-padded startup behavior and legal input domain. AP-002 already has accepted abstract evidence for these AP-stage claims; its implementation correspondence remains a later VO.
3. Preserve the proposed memory envelope assumptions as enforceable CON/DE caps. Independent review should examine attribution and overlap reasoning before treating the allocation as a credible AP budget. If reviewers find an unbounded charged category, bound it within the 2048-byte ceiling with positive margin.

No target WCET, completed firmware, linker map, physical consumption measurement, or flash build is required to reopen the AP selection gate. Those items remain later evidence for VO-002/003/004/005/006 and final F-001 comparison as applicable.

## Evidence boundary and unresolved risks

Inspected the AS spine projection; AS, unchanged F-001, VP-001 and VO-001 through VO-006; approved C-002 authority record and approval chain; both APs and resource envelopes; PR-002 accepted proof review and its gate metadata; AE-001/AE-002; and EP-001/EP-002 planning context. The VO-001 reconciliation receipt and pending independent request were inspected; no accepted receipt exists. No raw data, firmware, compiler output, linker map, stack report, target measurements, or flash image was inspected or produced. The gate outputs are structural eligibility checks only and do not verify the cited evidence.

Open risks include the assumed task/interrupt organization and caps, shared-memory ownership, category overlap, concrete stack use, C arithmetic correspondence, one-millisecond target timing, and unknown flash/cycle projections. All applicable VOs remain open. AE-001/002 and accepted theorem scope are unchanged.
