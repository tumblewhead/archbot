# AE-001: Evidence-grounded AP evaluation

## Disposition

**NO_DOWNSELECTION.** Neither candidate is established as satisfying every hard AS obligation. F-001 is applied to neither candidate. This evaluation does not modify AS-001 or F-001, select an architecture, approve firmware, or establish a target measurement.

## AS compliance matrix

`PASS` means the proposal and inspected evidence establish the obligation at the architecture's stated scope. `UNRESOLVED` means required evidence is absent; it is not a pass. Neither candidate has a `FAIL` established by the inspected artifacts.

## F-001 and tradeoffs

F-001 requires every hard C/R obligation to be met before comparing the Pareto vector (state SRAM bytes, worst-case cycles per update, flash bytes). Neither candidate clears that gate: both have unresolved C-002 and R-001; AP-001 also has unresolved R-002/R-003 at the candidate-specific proof level. Therefore no F scores, Pareto frontier, or preference are reported. The proposals' conceptual workload difference is not a substitute for target cycle data. No weights, criteria, or thresholds were changed.

## Minimum evidence to reopen downselection

1. Specify state and representation for each candidate, then provide a complete detector SRAM budget or target memory map demonstrating C-002.
2. Provide target WCET/cycle evidence showing each candidate can accept a sample every millisecond, under documented compiler, build, and measurement conditions.
3. For AP-001, provide a candidate-specific correctness/overflow argument covering zero-padded startup and every legal input sequence, plus concrete arithmetic-width and C mapping evidence.
4. For AP-002, map PR-002's ring initialization, oldest-slot/index convention, update order, expression widths/widening, and input range enforcement to a concrete implementation; address concurrency and reset behavior.
5. Measure/derive flash bytes for each feasible implementation to apply the unchanged F-001 Pareto comparison. If feasible candidates remain incomparable under F-001, obtain the documented stakeholder trade-off F-001 calls for.

No target SRAM, flash, cycles, or WCET measurements were present in inspected artifacts. The synthetic CSV fixture is explicitly non-authoritative and cannot establish universal correctness or target performance. [^results]
