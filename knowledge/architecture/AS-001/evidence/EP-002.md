# EP-002: Evidence for provisional AP selection

## Purpose and decision boundary

This plan identifies the minimum evidence needed to decide whether an AP is provisionally admissible for contract-first decomposition. It does not select an AP or establish final implementation acceptance. Provisional selection means that AP-stage evidence is adequate, later checks have named owners and due stages, and no binding requirement has been waived. Final acceptance still requires implementation and target-validation evidence required by AS-001 and EP-001.[^as][^vp][^ep1]

AE-001 remains historical evidence and is not overwritten. AE-002 records an unresolved AP-stage C-002 gate. EP-002 refines the evidence plan, not those evaluation dispositions.[^ae1][^ae2]

## Stage obligations

| Obligation | Assess at AP selection | May be deferred, with a carried VO | Not established by this plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-001 input domain | Confirm the declared 0..4095 unsigned domain used by both candidates. | No; assessed from the declared ADC source at this stage. | Physical ADC accuracy or sample-loss behavior outside the stated model. |
| C-002 SRAM | Provide a candidate-specific conservative envelope for the meaning of “detector state”; resolve scope before asserting compliance. | Concrete object/map and stack accounting may proceed to implementation after scope and an AP envelope are accepted. | A projected envelope is not measured RAM or proof of complete subsystem fit. |
| C-003 no FPU | Show integer-only architectural operations and carry build/toolchain confirmation. | Concrete binary inspection can be due at implementation. | Compiled firmware properties. |
| R-001 1 ms cadence | Bound work per update analytically and state assumptions for a plausible 1 ms implementation. This is a feasibility screen, not a target timing PASS. | Target WCET/representative timing and interference margin to target validation (VO-004). | Target timing, cycles, or deadline acceptance. |
| R-002 exact newest-32 sum, zero-padded startup | Require candidate-specific abstract algorithm evidence. AP-002 may rely on PR-002 only within its accepted ring/index/update assumptions; AP-001 needs its own argument. | Concrete source correspondence, reset/integration behavior and tests to implementation (VO-005). | Firmware correctness from abstract evidence alone. |
| R-003 no overflow | Require universal mathematical bounds for legal inputs and all algorithm-level intermediates. | Concrete C types, promotions, widening, and source correspondence to implementation (VO-006). | Implementation arithmetic safety without type review. |

An AP is not stage-admissible if evidence due now is `UNRESOLVED` or `VIOLATED`. Items deferred must have an owner, method, assumptions, acceptance criterion, due stage, and escalation path. Provisional selection is not a final pass on deferred obligations.[^vp]

## C-002 scope decision

AS-001 states “Detector state must fit within 2 KiB SRAM.” The target note calls 2 KiB a “detector subsystem” budget. VP-001 suggests including stack, interrupt context, and shared services where charged to the detector, but no authorized interpretation currently defines those as part of “detector state.” EP-002 therefore does **not** silently expand C-002 to those resources.[^as][^target][^vp]

For this plan, candidate envelopes first count detector-owned persistent algorithm state: sample history, accumulator, index/control state, and any other state explicitly introduced by that AP. Stack, interrupt context, shared services, and non-detector runtime allocations are separately identified and left outside the narrow subtotal pending an authorized scope interpretation. This accounting convention is an analytical view, not a change to AS and not a C-002 compliance finding. C-002 remains a decision blocker until an authorized interpretation states the charged boundary and the chosen AP has a complete conservative estimate against it.

## Candidate resource envelopes

Assumptions: 32 slots; samples represented as 16-bit unsigned values (sufficient for 0..4095); exact mathematical sum fits in 32 bits; 32-bit accumulator; 32-bit index/control allowance for conservative alignment-neutral accounting; no claim about compiler layout. AP-002 additionally needs a 32-bit square temporary if materialized; AP-001 needs a 32-bit recomputation accumulator. These are explicit modeling choices, not inspected declarations or measurements.[^adc][^ap1][^ap2][^pr2]

| Candidate | Persistent state analytical subtotal | Per-update transient / work state | Bound and uncertainty |
|---|---:|---|---|
| AP-001 recompute | 32 × 2 B history + 4 B control/index = **68 B** | 4 B accumulator = **72 B** modeled algorithm storage if accumulator is persistent; if automatic, stack contribution is separately unknown. Up to 32 square-and-accumulate terms/update. | Conservative allowance of a 32-bit control word; exact index representation and whether accumulator is persistent/automatic are unspecified. |
| AP-002 rolling | 32 × 2 B history + 4 B sum + 4 B control/index = **72 B** | 4 B square temporary, if materialized, gives **76 B** state-plus-temporary envelope. Constant-count arithmetic; the exact temporary may be register-allocated or stack-resident. | PR-002 bounds mathematical sum to 536,608,800 and add-before-subtract transient to 553,377,825; concrete expression types and storage remain open. |

Both modeled algorithm envelopes are far below 2048 B, leaving at least 1972 B in the AP-001 72 B model and 1972 B in the AP-002 76 B envelope. These residuals are arithmetic headroom under the stated narrow model, not a subsystem margin, measured result, or C-002 pass. A final AP gate needs a scope ruling and a complete accounting for every charged contributor. An unspecified term is not assumed to be zero.[^pr2][^target]

|---|---|---|---|

| C-002 RAM | 72 B modeled algorithm envelope | 76 B state-plus-temporary envelope | Scope ruling plus complete charged-boundary estimate; final map/stack evidence later. Current state: **BLOCKED / unresolved scope**. |

| R-001 cadence | At most 32 square/accumulate terms per update | Fixed-count eviction, square, and add/subtract update | Both have bounded algorithmic work. Relative update workload favors AP-002 analytically, but cycles/WCET and the 1 ms deadline await EP-001 target campaign. |
| R-002 exactness/startup | Candidate-specific invariant or direct finite-window argument required | PR-002 supplies abstract recurrence, initialization, wraparound under assumptions | AP-001 proof; AP-002 assumption correspondence. Concrete code/reset/integration evidence later. |
| R-003 overflow | Show 32-term bound and safe abstract intermediates | PR-002 abstract bound and update intermediate bound accepted | Both require explicit concrete integer type/expression review later. |
| F-001 comparative dimensions | State envelope above; flash and target cycles unknown | State envelope above; flash and target cycles unknown | Do not calculate a complete Pareto ranking until all hard requirements pass. No target measurement is inferred. |

## Differences relevant to F-001

F-001 compares state SRAM, worst-case cycles per update, and flash bytes after hard feasibility. The AP-001/AP-002 structural difference directly affects the cycle dimension: one recomputes up to 32 terms, the other performs a constant-count rolling update. Both retain the same 32-sample history, so the memory difference is small in the modeled algorithm state (the rolling sum and control word offset any transient accounting difference). Neither proposal supplies flash implementation size, and neither has target cycle evidence. Therefore operation count can inform feasibility planning but cannot establish a Pareto winner. Flash bytes, actual cycles/WCET, and measured memory remain in EP-001's paired target-level campaign after concrete implementation exists.[^f][^ap1][^ap2][^ep1]

## F-001 interpretation

F-001 currently makes all AS constraints and requirements hard-feasibility conditions and says unknowns are `UNRESOLVED`; it does not define stage-specific evidence meanings. EP-002 does not alter its thresholds, dimensions, or ranking policy. Before an evaluator treats conditional AP evidence for R-001 or later-due implementation checks as sufficient for provisional selection, an authorized review must either approve a stage-specific interpretation/addendum to F-001 or issue a versioned F-001 revision. Until then, do not use stage-specific relaxation to call all-obligation feasibility or apply F-001 as though final unknowns were passes. F-001's Pareto comparison remains for final feasible candidates per EP-001.[^f][^ep1]

## Outstanding verification obligations

Carry existing VO-001 through VO-006 unchanged. No VO is discharged by this plan. At minimum, selected AP handoff retains:

- **VO-001 / C-001:** domain source and assumption trace; due at AS assessment.
- **VO-002 / C-002:** authorized meaning of detector state, candidate complete envelope, then implementation map/static accounting and applicable stack analysis.
- **VO-003 / C-003:** confirm no floating-point reliance in concrete build.
- **VO-004 / R-001:** target WCET/cycle evidence and 1 ms margin at target validation.
- **VO-005 / R-002:** implementation correspondence, initialization/reset, indexing, synchronization, and integration evidence.
- **VO-006 / R-003:** actual operand types, promotions, widening before multiplication, and accumulator/update correspondence.

Also carry the open decision on F-001's approved stage-specific interpretation. Deferred status requires a credible method, owner, assumptions, acceptance criteria, and due gate; the presence of an existing VO file alone does not establish that its current plan is sufficient.[^vo1][^vo2][^vo3][^vo4][^vo5][^vo6][^f]

## Smallest defensible evidence package and staged policy

1. **Scope and budget sheet:** obtain an authorized C-002 boundary interpretation; document candidate-specific state layout assumptions and conservative totals; leave unbounded charged terms unresolved.
2. **Algorithm evidence:** candidate-specific universal correctness/overflow argument for AP-001; for AP-002, use PR-002's accepted abstract claims with an explicit checklist matching ring initialization, oldest-slot convention, update order and range assumptions. Independently review both.
3. **Stage-defer register:** attach VOs 002–006 with owner, method, acceptance criterion, due gate and escalation; explicitly distinguish estimated from measured claims.
4. **F-001 authority:** approve a stage-specific interpretation or versioned revision before using provisional-stage dispositions in its hard-feasibility rule. Preserve its existing three ranking dimensions and Pareto policy.

**Proposed selection policy:** first assess AP-stage evidence for all obligations, requiring evidence now and credible carry-forward plans for final checks due later. If C-002 scope remains ambiguous or any AP-due evidence is unresolved, record `NO_DOWNSELECTION`. After the interpretation is approved and stage-admissible candidates are identified, provisional AP selection may be made under an approved stage-specific F-001 reading, with every VO attached as a condition of progression into CON. Final implementation acceptance and any final ranking that needs measured F-001 values wait for EP-001's target campaign. Do not use a future due date as a stage PASS or represent projected resources as measured results.[^f][^vp][^ep1]

## Decision blockers and recommended next gate

**Blockers:** (1) C-002 has no authorized scope interpretation; (2) F-001 lacks an approved stage-specific interpretation for provisional selection while retaining hard conditions and final Pareto policy; (3) AP-001 candidate-specific mathematical evidence is absent from the inspected material; (4) candidate-complete resource envelopes and AP-002 assumption matching require submission/review. These block an evidence-backed AP selection, not future CON work after the blockers are resolved.

**Recommended next gate:** upstream review of C-002 scope and F-001 stage interpretation, followed by candidate evidence review against this minimum package. Do not select an AP in EP-002. Preserve EP-001 unchanged for final target validation and comparative SRAM/cycle/flash evidence.[^ae2][^ep1]
