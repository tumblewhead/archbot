# DE-AP2-INGRESS-v6 placeholder

**Owner:** implementation-agent (assignment pending). Own one-to-one
delivery of accepted legal samples to the update boundary. The publisher
owns uninterrupted ordered delivery as an external unverified assumption.
The contract defines no invalid-input response, transport, sequence/loss
detection, or platform mechanism.
