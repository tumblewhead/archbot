# Incremental approach

Maintain a 32-element ring buffer and a rolling squared sum. On an update, subtract the exiting squared sample and add the incoming squared sample. Conceptual workload: two squarings and constant additional arithmetic per update. Requires proof that updates preserve the exact rolling invariant and avoid intermediate underflow/overflow.
