# C-002-SCOPE-001: approved interpretation

The engineering authority selected **Option B** on 2026-10-12 (America/Los_Angeles).
The scope of `C-002` is **total detector-attributable SRAM**, limited to **2048 bytes**.

The authorized boundary includes persistent state, temporary or scratch memory,
detector-charged call stack, detector-attributable interrupt context, applicable
shared allocations, bounded dynamic allocations (if used), and other directly
attributable SRAM. Count overlapping allocations once, and establish the peak
for the worst permitted simultaneous activity. Any excluded/shared allocation
needs a stated ownership/attribution rationale. A charge cannot silently be zero.

At AP selection a **credible categorized budget with positive margin** is sufficient
for the *stage-level* check if its assumptions, enforcement plan, and risks are
explicit. It is not a measurement. Later CON/DE allocation and implementation
must verify adherence to that budget; `VO-002` stays open until accepted evidence.

This is an **interpretation** of C-002's coverage, **not an amendment of its
numeric limit**. It does not modify the final Figure of Merit `F-001`, which
continues to rank **state SRAM**, cycles, and flash. C-002 feasibility and F-001
state-memory ranking are deliberately distinct metrics.

Authority provenance: `process/decisions/C-002-approval.md` (project-controlled
copy of the explicit authority statement). Machine-readable record:
`process/decisions/C-002-scope.yaml`.
