## The engineering hinge

Correct numeric output is not the whole observable relation. The
information needed for the *next* numeric energy, for an *old owed*
result and for its *consumer-visible sequence/rank identity* can live
at different boundaries and have different lifetimes. A closed
31-sample numeric state alone does not automatically preserve unpaid
result debt. A physically tiny local detector can still depend on an
expensive upstream snapshot and index service.

This is why the next independent evaluation must keep three layers
separate:

- The numeric kernel: do A/B/C maintain exact future-window energy
  under their explicit preconditions?
- The interface: can a real consumer obtain exact `(n,E_n)`, with
  rejection, reset and owed-result semantics?
- Realization and objective: which owned/shared/external resources
  are retained, and what comparable RAM, operation effort and device
  electrical energy actually follow?

The first layer has conditional formulas and finite model probes.
The second lacks a physical contract for all candidates. The third
has symbolic resource expressions but no target measurements.

## What this authorization does and does not mean

The accepted R1 clarifies that acknowledgment is OPTIONAL; observable
correct reset ordering and old/new association are mandatory. It
does not make RI, RN, C-HIST, retention, retry, bounded epochs,
publisher backpressure or hardware qualification available.

Stage 9 permits a fresh independent evaluation of A/B/C using exact
synthetic inputs, independent finite models and symbolic comparisons.
It cannot choose A or B because of arithmetic simplicity, nor C
because external RAM looks smaller. Unknown F values must remain
unknown, and actual target claims need future target evidence.
